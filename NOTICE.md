## The example course (an adaptation)

The course in `examples/linear-algebra-done-right/` (knowledge graph, lessons, worked examples, practice and quiz
questions, hints, solutions and visuals) is an **adaptation** of the book, made with Universal Academy. It was
generated from the book's pages by large language models and partly edited by hand.

Changes made to the original:

- the chapter's material was split into small skills linked by prerequisites (the knowledge graph);
- the text was rewritten as lessons, with new explanations, worked examples and visuals;
- new exercises, questions, hints and solutions were written;
- page references point back to the book.

The course is not the book's text, may contain mistakes the book does not have, and is not reviewed or endorsed by
Sheldon Axler or Springer. The adaptation is licensed under
[CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) as well, noncommercial use only.
